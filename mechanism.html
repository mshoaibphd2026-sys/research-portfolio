Charge Transfer & Mechanism

My mechanistic studies focused on identifying the reactive species involved in photocatalytic oxidation and understanding how charge carriers move across semiconductor interfaces.

Radical Trapping Experiments

Radical trapping experiments were used to identify the dominant reactive species involved in the photocatalytic oxidation process. By selectively suppressing specific reactive intermediates, the experiments helped clarify which charge carriers and radical species played the major role in the reaction pathway.

Proposed Charge-Transfer Mechanism

The proposed mechanism combines the scavenger results with band alignment, electrochemical measurements, and spectroscopic evidence to explain interfacial electron transfer, charge separation, and the generation of reactive species during photocatalysis.

Mechanistic Tools

The charge-transfer pathway was investigated using radical trapping experiments, Mott–Schottky analysis, electrochemical impedance spectroscopy, photocurrent measurements, XPS, and complementary electronic-structure analysis.
