0D/1D Heterostructures

My research focused on the design and synthesis of semiconductor heterostructures by coupling zero-dimensional metal oxide nanostructures with one-dimensional sodium titanate nanotubes.

Hydrothermal Synthesis

Hydrothermal synthesis was used to promote controlled nucleation and growth of semiconductor nanostructures under elevated temperature and pressure. Sodium titanate nanotubes served as the one-dimensional support for the in situ growth of metal oxide nanoclusters, enabling intimate interfacial contact and the formation of 0D/1D heterostructures.

Materials Design Strategy

The heterostructure design aimed to improve interfacial charge separation, enhance visible-light utilization, and create favorable pathways for charge transfer during photocatalytic reactions.

Characterization

The synthesized materials were characterized using XRD, TEM/HRTEM, SEM, XPS, Raman spectroscopy, UV–Vis diffuse reflectance spectroscopy, electrochemical impedance spectroscopy, Mott–Schottky analysis, and photocurrent measurements.
